# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

## Additional Delivery & Cross-functional Personas

These additional personas clarify ownership for technical decisions, security review, stakeholder alignment, release readiness, and operational feedback. They help OctoAcme teams reduce ambiguity, improve handoffs, and make project execution more consistent.

### Technical Lead

#### Role Summary

Technical Leads guide technical direction and help ensure that implementation decisions align with architecture, maintainability, and delivery goals.

#### Responsibilities

- Support architecture and design decisions
- Review complex technical plans and implementation approaches
- Help developers resolve technical blockers
- Coordinate with Project Managers and Product Managers on scope, risk, and trade-offs
- Promote engineering standards and maintainability

#### Goals

- Improve technical consistency across project work
- Reduce delivery risk caused by unclear technical ownership
- Support reliable and scalable implementation decisions

#### Typical Communication

- Technical design discussions
- Pull request and architecture reviews
- Risk and dependency conversations with project leads

---

### Security Reviewer

#### Role Summary

Security Reviewers help identify and reduce security risks during planning, implementation, review, and release activities.

#### Responsibilities

- Review security-sensitive changes
- Support secure development practices
- Help interpret CI security scan results
- Coordinate with Developers and QA on remediation steps
- Escalate security concerns when risk exceeds project tolerance

#### Goals

- Reduce security risk before release
- Improve confidence in production readiness
- Ensure security considerations are part of normal delivery workflows

#### Typical Communication

- Security review comments
- CI/security scan findings
- Risk register updates and escalation notes

---

### UX or Design Representative

#### Role Summary

UX or Design Representatives provide usability and design guidance for user-facing workflows and help ensure that solutions meet user needs.

#### Responsibilities

- Validate user-facing flows before implementation
- Provide design input and usability feedback
- Collaborate with Product Managers on user expectations and acceptance criteria
- Support Developers during implementation when design clarification is needed

#### Goals

- Improve usability and user experience
- Reduce rework caused by unclear design expectations
- Align product outcomes with real user needs

#### Typical Communication

- Design reviews
- User workflow notes
- Feedback during planning, demos, and acceptance reviews

---

### Stakeholder or Business Sponsor

#### Role Summary

Stakeholders or Business Sponsors represent business priorities, validate success metrics, and support escalation when decisions affect scope, timeline, or outcomes.

#### Responsibilities

- Confirm business goals and expected outcomes
- Validate success metrics and project priorities
- Provide input during major decision gates
- Support escalation when trade-offs require business approval

#### Goals

- Keep project work aligned with business value
- Improve decision-making during scope or timeline changes
- Ensure outcomes are measurable and relevant

#### Typical Communication

- Project one-pager review
- Milestone updates
- Decision gate approvals and escalation conversations

---

### Release Coordinator

#### Role Summary

Release Coordinators help prepare and coordinate releases so deployments are predictable, communicated, and reversible when needed.

#### Responsibilities

- Coordinate release readiness activities
- Confirm release notes, deployment plans, and rollback plans
- Communicate release timing and impact
- Support post-deployment verification

#### Goals

- Reduce release risk
- Improve deployment coordination
- Ensure release activities are repeatable and traceable

#### Typical Communication

- Release checklists
- Deployment readiness updates
- Post-release verification notes

---

### Operations or Support Representative

#### Role Summary

Operations or Support Representatives provide feedback from production, incidents, support tickets, and user-facing operational concerns.

#### Responsibilities

- Share operational risks and support trends
- Provide feedback from incidents or recurring user issues
- Help validate production readiness from a support perspective
- Coordinate with Project Managers, Developers, and QA on operational follow-up

#### Goals

- Improve production stability
- Reduce repeated support issues
- Feed operational learnings back into planning and retrospectives

#### Typical Communication

- Support summaries
- Incident follow-up notes
- Retrospective inputs and operational readiness feedback
---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation
